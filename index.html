Wild Sit Down Uploader

A personal command-line tool that uploads finished videos to its operator's own YouTube channel.

What it does

This is a single-user desktop tool. It takes a video file that the operator has already produced on their own computer, together with a title, description and tags, and uploads it to the operator's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

Who uses it

Only its operator, on their own machine, for their own channel. It is not distributed, not offered as a service, and has no other users or accounts.

What access it requests

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — the single scope required to upload a video to the signed-in channel.

It does not read, modify or delete existing videos, and requests no other Google data.
